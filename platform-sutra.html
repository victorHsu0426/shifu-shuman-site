六祖壇經講座｜見額法師

七集・約 7 小時 45 分鐘・2026 年 4–7 月・台中大里講授

《六祖壇經》是禪宗最重要的經典之一，記錄東土六祖惠能的生平與說法，核心在「見性成佛」與「自性本來清淨」。本系列由見額法師依《壇經》逐品講授，每集附章節目錄與完整逐字稿（預設收合），點擊即可跳到 YouTube 對應段落。

The Platform Sutra (六祖壇經) is one of the central texts of Chan (Zen) Buddhism. It records the life and teachings of the Sixth Patriarch, Huineng, and centers on seeing one's own nature and the originally pure self-nature. Venerable Jian E taught this seven-part series in Taichung between April and July 2026: about 7 hours 45 minutes in total, with chapter outlines and full transcripts for every episode.

七集目錄（依集數順序）

第1集

見性成佛與頓悟法門

教授日期 2026/04/23・約 60 分鐘

本集開宗明義說明「見性成佛」。佛法即在世間，修行是在選佛場中練習。透過「以手指月」「過河不須舟」等禪宗譬喻，提醒不要執著文字名相；並辨析自性、本性、佛性、法性的異同，最後回到「當下成就法身佛」。

Episode 1 opens with the core idea of seeing one's own nature and attaining Buddhahood. Through Chan images such as "pointing at the moon with a finger" and "no need for a boat once across the river," it explains why one should not cling to words, and clarifies the terms nature, Buddha-nature and Dharma-nature.

  • 課程開宗明義：見性成佛與頓悟法門
  • 佛法在世間——修行的選佛場
  • 修心指南：從慣性反應到智慧回應
第2集

禪宗核心思想

教授日期 2026/04/30・約 57 分鐘

本集以「認識法身佛」「自性福田」「頓悟法門」為主軸，說明頓悟不在快慢，而在當下的覺性。講解《壇經》的「無念為宗、無相為體、無住為本」，並以「隨處皆道場」與「隨處皆修羅場」對照生活禪的實踐。

Episode 2 centers on the Dharma body, the field of self-nature and sudden awakening, which is present awareness rather than speed. It explains the Platform Sutra's essence: no-thought as the teaching, no-form as the substance, no-abiding as the foundation, and contrasts a living Chan practice that makes every place a field of awakening with one that makes it a battlefield.

  • 認識法身佛：禪宗的當下證悟
  • 自性福田：打破心外求法的心地法門
  • 頓悟法門：非關快慢的當下覺性
第3集

惠能大師生平

教授日期 2026/05/14・約 69 分鐘

本集依《壇經·行由品》講述六祖惠能的生平：出生瑞相、童年目不識丁仍能開悟，聽聞《金剛經》後北上黃梅參禮五祖。並談碓坊舂米、夜傳衣缽、混跡獵人隊的故事，最後說到修行的小隱、中隱與大隱，以及風幡之辯與一花開五葉。

Episode 3 traces the life of the Sixth Patriarch Huineng through the Chapter on Origins: his auspicious birth, an unschooled childhood in which he still awakened, his awakening on hearing the Diamond Sutra, his journey north to Huangmei to meet the Fifth Patriarch, the years at the mill, the night transmission of the robe, and the lessons of the wind and the banner.

  • 東土六祖簡介
  • 六祖的生卒年與歷史記載的落差
  • 惠能大師的出生瑞相與命名由來
第4集

各品大綱

教授日期 2026/05/21・約 77 分鐘

本集逐品概述《六祖壇經》的結構：從行由、般若、疑問三品，到定慧一體與坐禪的重新定義、無相心地戒、應機施教，以及法無頓漸、三十六對法與自在入滅。也說明各版本背景，幫助初學者先建立整體地圖。

Episode 4 gives a chapter-by-chapter map of the Platform Sutra: from Origins, Prajna and Questions, through the unity of concentration and wisdom, the formless precept of the mind-ground, skillful teaching, to the Thirty-six Pairs and the final passing. It also sets out the background of the editions, giving beginners an overall picture.

  • 《六祖壇經》各品大綱與不同版本背景
  • 前三品：行由品、般若品、疑問品
  • 第四、五品：定慧一體與坐禪的重新定義
第5集

壇經版本流傳

教授日期 2026/07/09・約 58 分鐘

本集梳理《壇經》版本的流傳：從法海本與《六祖法寶記》的原始筆記，到敦煌藏經洞發現的敦煌系統本，再到北宋惠昕本、契嵩本，最後是元代宗寶本，也就是今日最通行、並收入《大正藏》的版本。

Episode 5 traces how the Platform Sutra was transmitted in different editions: the early notes preserved by Fahai and the Record of the Dharma Treasure, the Dunhuang manuscripts found in the Mogao caves, the Northern Song editions of Huixin and Qisong, and the Yuan edition by Zongbao, the most widely circulated text today.

  • 《壇經》版本流傳演進概述
  • 原始筆記：法海本與《六祖法寶記》
  • 敦煌藏經洞歷史與敦煌系統本特色
第6集

版本因緣與頓教核心

教授日期 2026/07/16・約 70 分鐘

本集從「不立文字為何需要《壇經》」切入，說明文字如標月之指，並介紹經典校勘與 CBETA 電子佛典。接著解說〈行由第一〉的大梵寺說法因緣、「六成就」與稱「經」的涵義，以及頓教核心：自性本來清淨，用此心直了成佛。

Episode 6 asks why a text is needed if the teaching lies beyond words, explains words as the finger that points to the moon, and introduces critical editions and the CBETA digital canon. It then covers the setting of the sermon at Dafan Temple, the meaning of calling it a sutra, and the core of sudden teaching: self-nature is originally pure, and one may awaken directly by means of this very mind.

  • 《六祖壇經》版本因緣
  • 不立文字為何需《壇經》？文字即標月之指
  • 經典版本校勘與 CBETA 電子佛典
第7集

聞金剛經與自性福田

教授日期 2026/07/23・約 75 分鐘

本集從惠能聞《金剛經》而開悟講起，談持《金剛經》見性成佛與「自性天真佛」，說明佛性的三大定義與人人皆具覺性。述惠能千里投黃梅、「人雖有南北，佛性本無南北」，以及五祖令其破柴踏碓的用意，即「自性福田」。

Episode 7 begins with Huineng hearing the Diamond Sutra and goes on to the three definitions of Buddha-nature and the claim that all beings have an awakened nature. It recounts his journey to Huangmei, the line "people have north and south, but Buddha-nature has no north or south," and the Fifth Patriarch's purpose in having him split wood and pound rice, the field of self-nature.

  • 惠能聞《金剛經》之因緣
  • 持《金剛經》見性成佛與自性天真佛
  • 佛性的三大定義與人人皆具覺性

常見問題 FAQ

六祖壇經是什麼？

《六祖壇經》是禪宗最重要的經典之一，記錄六祖惠能的生平與說法，核心是見性成佛與「自性本來清淨」。

What is the Platform Sutra?
The Platform Sutra is one of the central texts of Chan (Zen) Buddhism. It records the life and teachings of the Sixth Patriarch Huineng, and its core is seeing one's own nature and the originally pure self-nature.

惠能是誰？

惠能（638–713）是中國禪宗的第六祖。出身貧寒、不識字，聽聞《金剛經》而開悟，之後北上黃梅參禮五祖弘忍，被後世尊為禪宗的實際奠基者。

Who was Huineng?
Huineng (638–713) was the Sixth Patriarch of Chan in China. Born poor and unschooled, he awakened on hearing the Diamond Sutra, later traveled north to Huangmei to study with the Fifth Patriarch Hongren, and is revered as the effective founder of Chan.

什麼是「見性成佛」？

見性是見到自己的本性。當下覺悟到自性本來清淨、本具佛性，即能成佛，不必假借外求。

What does "seeing one's nature and becoming a Buddha" mean?
Seeing one's nature means realizing one's own original nature. When one awakens in the present to the originally pure, Buddha-endowed self-nature, one becomes a Buddha, without seeking it outside.

《壇經》有哪些版本？

主要有敦煌本、北宋惠昕本、契嵩本，以及元代宗寶本。今日最通行的是宗寶本，並收入《大正藏》。第 5 集有完整說明。

What editions of the Platform Sutra are there?
The main ones are the Dunhuang manuscripts, the Northern Song edition of Huixin, the edition of Qisong, and the Yuan edition by Zongbao. The Zongbao edition is the most widely circulated today and is included in the Taishō canon. Episode 5 explains them in full.

第一次聽應該從哪一集開始？

建議依集數 1 至 7 順序聽。若只想先了解大意，可先看第 1 集（見性成佛）與第 4 集（各品大綱）。

Where should a first-time listener start?
Listen in order from Episode 1 to 7. If you only want the overall picture first, start with Episode 1 (seeing one's nature) and Episode 4 (the outline of the chapters).
