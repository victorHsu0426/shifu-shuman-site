English summary

Episode 7 begins with Huineng hearing the Diamond Sutra and goes on to the three definitions of Buddha-nature and the claim that all beings have an awakened nature. It recounts his journey to Huangmei, the line "people have north and south, but Buddha-nature has no north or south," and the Fifth Patriarch's purpose in having him split wood and pound rice, the field of self-nature.
