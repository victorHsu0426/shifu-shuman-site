本集摘要

本集梳理《壇經》版本的流傳：從法海本與《六祖法寶記》的原始筆記，到敦煌藏經洞發現的敦煌系統本，再到北宋惠昕本、契嵩本，最後是元代宗寶本，也就是今日最通行、並收入《大正藏》的版本。

English summary

Episode 5 traces how the Platform Sutra was transmitted in different editions: the early notes preserved by Fahai and the Record of the Dharma Treasure, the Dunhuang manuscripts found in the Mogao caves, the Northern Song editions of Huixin and Qisong, and the Yuan edition by Zongbao, the most widely circulated text today.
