English summary

Episode 3 traces the life of the Sixth Patriarch Huineng through the Chapter on Origins: his auspicious birth, an unschooled childhood in which he still awakened, his awakening on hearing the Diamond Sutra, his journey north to Huangmei to meet the Fifth Patriarch, the years at the mill, the night transmission of the robe, and the lessons of the wind and the banner.
