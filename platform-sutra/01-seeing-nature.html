English summary

Episode 1 opens with the core idea of seeing one's own nature and attaining Buddhahood. Through Chan images such as "pointing at the moon with a finger" and "no need for a boat once across the river," it explains why one should not cling to words, and clarifies the terms nature, Buddha-nature and Dharma-nature.
