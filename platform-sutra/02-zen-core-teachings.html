本集摘要

本集以「認識法身佛」「自性福田」「頓悟法門」為主軸，說明頓悟不在快慢，而在當下的覺性。講解《壇經》的「無念為宗、無相為體、無住為本」，並以「隨處皆道場」與「隨處皆修羅場」對照生活禪的實踐。

English summary

Episode 2 centers on the Dharma body, the field of self-nature and sudden awakening, which is present awareness rather than speed. It explains the Platform Sutra's essence: no-thought as the teaching, no-form as the substance, no-abiding as the foundation, and contrasts a living Chan practice that makes every place a field of awakening with one that makes it a battlefield.
